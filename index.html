    Clinical guidelines

    General CDC / ADA prevention-program targets

    Aerobic activity

    At least 150 minutes per week of moderate-intensity activity (e.g. brisk walking), spread across 3 or more days, with no more than 2 consecutive days off.

    Carbohydrate tracking

    Favor consistent, moderate carbohydrate intake per meal, prioritizing fiber-rich whole grains, legumes, and non-starchy vegetables over refined sugars.

    Weight target

    A 5–7% reduction in body weight is the standard prevention-program goal shown to lower progression risk toward type 2 diabetes.

    Source: CDC National Diabetes Prevention Program. Consult a clinician before changing diet or exercise routines.
